Common questions

How do I join my family’s board?

Open the invite link a family member sent you on your iPhone, or tap “I have an invite” and type the 6-character code.

I didn’t get my sign-in code

Check your spam folder and that the email is spelled right. You can ask for a new code after 30 seconds. Codes expire after 10 minutes.

I’m not getting alerts

In the app go to Settings → Alerts and make sure they’re on. Then check iPhone Settings → Notifications → What Time’s the Game?!, and your quiet hours in the app.

Who can change games?

The Owner, and Editors for the kids they were assigned. Viewers can see games, say “I’m going” and get alerts.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. If you own a board, hand it to someone else or delete the board first. Our privacy policy explains what gets deleted.
