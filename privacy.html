Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026

What Time’s the Game?! (“the app”) is a shared schedule for a family’s kids’ sports. It is published by Norma Dominguez in the United States (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. If you have a question, write to norma.d.dominguez@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We only keep what the app needs to show your family’s games and tell you when they change.
  • No ads, no selling or renting of your data, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Only the people in your family board can see your board.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: Settings → Delete account.

What we collect

Account. Your email address (to send you a 6-digit sign-in code) or, if you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier and email Apple gives us (this can be an Apple “Hide My Email” address). The name you want the family to see, and a profile photo if you choose to add one.

Your family board. The board name, who is in it and each person’s role (Owner, Editor or Viewer), invites you send (the invite code and, if you invite by email, that email address), and which kids each person follows.

Kids and games. What parents type in: each kid’s first name and color, and each game’s sport, type, date, start and end time, place and notes. Also the history of changes to a game, who said “I’m going”, and “thanks” messages.

Alerts. If you turn on notifications, your phone’s push notification token, your alert settings and quiet hours, and the list of alerts we sent you.

Calendar. If you add a game to your iPhone Calendar, that happens on your phone; we don’t read your calendar. If you turn on the calendar subscription, we create a private link that your calendar uses to fetch the games of the kids you follow.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, payment information, or any data for advertising. The app has no analytics or advertising tools.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure (for example, limiting wrong-code attempts).
  • To show your family’s schedule to the people in your board.
  • To send push notifications when a game you follow is added, changed or cancelled, and the reminders you allow. Each type can be turned off in Settings.
  • To answer you when you contact support.

Who can see your information

Your family board. People in your board see the board’s kids, games, names, profile photos, who is going and the change history. What each person can change depends on their role. These rules are enforced on our servers, not only in the app.

Service providers that run the app for us, only to provide it:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, sending sign-in emails, and photo storage.
  • Expo: delivering push notifications to your phone.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and Apple’s push notification service.

We don’t sell or share your personal information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Kids’ information

The app is made for parents and other adult relatives. Kids don’t use it and don’t have accounts. The only information about kids is what a parent or relative enters: a first name and the game schedule. The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. Your profile, photo, settings and push tokens are deleted. If you own a board, you first hand it to someone else or delete the board.
  • Delete a board: the Owner can delete it, which removes its kids, games and history.
  • Deleted data is removed from our systems within 30 days, including backups.
  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. Invites expire, and the Owner can revoke them.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored with our database provider. Access is limited by board and role. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if something happens that affects you.

Your choices and rights

You can see and edit your name, photo and alert settings in the app, and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to norma.d.dominguez@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for asking.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
