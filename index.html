For the whole family

Some keep the schedule. Everyone else just shows up.

If you keep the schedule

Owner and editors

  • Start a family board in a minute: name it, add the kids, add the next game.
  • Invite relatives with a link or a 6-character code.
  • Editors only change the games of the kids assigned to them.
  • After saving, you see “Sent to 6 family members” and who’s going.

If you just want to be there

Grandparents, aunts, uncles

  • Nothing to set up. Open the invite, sign in, done.
  • Choose which kids you follow and skip the rest.
  • Tap “I’m going” so the parents know you’ll be in the stands.
  • Get a heads-up only when something changes.

Questions

Good to know

Who can add or change games?

The board owner and the editors they choose. Each editor can only change the games of the kids assigned to them. Everyone else can see the games, say “I’m going” and get alerts.

Does Grandma need to set anything up?

No. She opens the invite, signs in with Apple or her email, picks which grandkids to follow and she’s in.

What happens when a game changes?

Everyone who follows that kid gets one alert with the new time or place and what it was before. The change also shows in Updates.

Can I see games in my own calendar?

Yes. Add a single game from its page, or subscribe once and the games of the kids you follow stay up to date in your iPhone calendar.

Is it on Android?

The app launches on iPhone first. Android comes later.
